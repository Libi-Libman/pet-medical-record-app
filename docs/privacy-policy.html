Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

This Privacy Policy explains how the pet medical record app ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is built and maintained by an independent developer, not a company. It exists to help you keep your pet's medical history organized, show a summary to your vet, and track upcoming appointments and ongoing treatments. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password, used to create and sign in to your account. Your password is never stored in plain text.

  • Pet profile information: name, species, breed, sex, birth date, sterilization status, and microchip number.

  • Medical record information: health conditions and episodes, clinical events and vet visits, medications and dosing schedules, allergies, and your vet's contact details.

  • Pet photos: optional images you choose to upload for a pet's profile.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or any data from your device beyond what you enter directly into the App.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to operate the App's core features: storing and displaying your pet's medical history, generating the vet-visit summary card, and reminding you about upcoming appointments and ongoing treatments.

We do not use your information for advertising, and the App contains no ad networks. We do not use any third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, so your usage of the App is not tracked or profiled. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your information is stored using Supabase, a database and backend provider, hosted in a data center in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Data is encrypted in transit between your device and our servers.

Access to your data is restricted by row-level security rules so that you can only view and edit your own pets and records. We do not maintain a separate backend server — the App communicates directly with our Supabase project.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing. The only party with access to your data is Supabase, our database hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of the App, its users, or the public.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual pets, medications, allergies, timeline entries, and other records at any time directly within the App, and those deletions take effect immediately.

If you want your entire account and all associated data permanently deleted, you can request this directly within the App from Settings > Delete account. We will process the deletion within 30 days and send you a confirmation email once it's complete. We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active, or until you request deletion.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights and Choices

Because your data is stored in the EU, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

  • Correct inaccurate data (most fields can be edited directly in the App)

  • Delete your data, in whole or in part (see "Data Retention and Deletion" above)

  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
